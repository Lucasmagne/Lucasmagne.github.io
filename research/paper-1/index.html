SayWhen: Set-Valued Semantic Commitment for Low-Regret Early Speech

View on OpenReview

About the Paper

SayWhen explores how voice agents can begin responding before a user has finished speaking without making a risky or incorrect commitment. Instead of betting on one predicted ending, the system compares multiple possible continuations, identifies a task-directed action they share, and releases speech only after a semantic safety gate confirms that later transcript updates have not invalidated it. The paper evaluates this approach through branching dialogue tests and full-duplex retail simulations, showing how agents can reduce response delay while keeping public commitments accurate and low-regret.
